# About NaniSoft

NaniSoft is run by working cybersecurity practitioners who spend their days
building, breaking, and defending systems — and their evenings writing it down.
This site is where we publish what we learn so that the next team doesn't have to
start from zero.

We cover the full security stack — IAM, application security, network security,
and governance — plus the AI × Cyber frontier that's reshaping all of it. The
writing here is practitioner-first: grounded in real programs, opinionated where
it matters, and honest about what we don't yet know.

## What we believe

- **Security exists to let the business move, not to gate it.** The best control
  is the one your engineers actually use and your auditors can actually verify.
- **AI changes both sides of the equation.** Defenders get faster; attackers do
  too. Treating AI as only a tool, or only a threat, misses half the picture.
- **Governance is leverage.** A clear risk picture and a sane control set make
  every other security decision faster — not slower.
- **Write it down.** Knowledge that lives in one head doesn't scale. We publish
  so the work compounds.

## How to work with us

We partner with organizations that need senior security leadership without the
overhead of a full-time hire, or that need a team to actually stand up and
operationalize a program.

### vCISO & Security Advisory

Fractional or interim security leadership — risk strategy, board-level
reporting, and a clear priorities roadmap for your context.

### Control Setup & Implementation

We don't just write the policy. We stand up the controls — IAM, detection, SDLC,
network — and make them run in your environment.

### AI-Era Defense

AI for Cyber and Cyber for AI, applied: secure your AI stack, and put AI to work
in your detection and response program without naive trust.

### Program Design & Audit Readiness

Mapping to ISO 27001, SOC 2, or NIST CSF, gap assessments, and audit-ready
evidence — built so it survives the next audit, not just the next one.
