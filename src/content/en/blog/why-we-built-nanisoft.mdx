  <h2 id="the-gap">The gap</h2>

  Large organizations don't buy their security stack in one piece. They inherit an
  enterprise suite — authentication, authorization, endpoint, VPN, network security
  — and then they build. Every gap that suite leaves uncovered becomes an internal
  tool, a custom integration, or a team that holds the answer in their head.

  That work is real, and it is expensive. It is also fragile: the people who built
  it move on, the assumptions age, and the gaps quietly reopen.

  <h2 id="the-answer">The answer</h2>

  Nanisoft exists to replace that custom build with one cohesive suite. Instead of
  a stack of point tools that each solve one problem and leave the seams exposed,
  we read from a single live graph of every path into a system — ingress, identity,
  traversal, data — so the route an attacker would take is visible before they
  take it.

  <h2 id="the-first-chapter">The first chapter</h2>

  This first chapter is the Core trio — **Atlas**, **Bedrock**, and **Keystone** —
  plus four more categories of tooling, twenty-one products in all. More is coming,
  and we will keep the changelog honest about what shipped and what is next.
