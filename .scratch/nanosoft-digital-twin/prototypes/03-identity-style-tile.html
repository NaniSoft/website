01 / wordmark

The mark, and the flow through it

Three rough directions for the nanisoft lockup. All share one idea: a connection that is alive. The wordmark is Satoshi 700, tight tracking. The graph glyph is the "i dot" expressed as a node.

W1   node + flow

The "i" dot becomes a ringed graph node; a live connection trails right. The accent (jade) is reserved for the active link only.

W2   continuous stroke

A single flow line runs through the wordmark, bookended by two live nodes. Quietest of the three. Pairs with a small wordmark-only footer lockup.

W3   monogram lockup

A monogram "n" built from connected nodes (one live, one ringed) beside the wordmark. Best for favicons, app bars, the playground dock.

02 / palette

One accent, locked everywhere

Petrol is the deep base (the lake). Bone is the light surface. Teal is the secondary signal; jade is the single accent, used only for the live, the active, the now. No purple, no neon, no pure black or white.

03 / typography

Satoshi for voice, JetBrains Mono for data

A humanist grotesk for the narrative voice and a precise mono for the twin's data: table names, node IDs, query fragments. Italic of the same family carries emphasis. No serif.

04 / motion language

The twin is alive. The motion says so.

Four principles govern every moving thing. Motion is motivated, never decorative. The hero above is the reference. Click the panel to feel the ripple.

click anywhere

prefers-reduced-motion: the graph holds still, ripples are disabled, reveals are instant.

breathe

Idle is not dead

Nodes drift on a slow sine and pulse their radius a few percent. The twin looks alive even with no input. Period 4 to 6 seconds. Never linear.

traverse

Data flows along edges

A bright dot travels each edge on a staggered cycle, link glow ramps then fades. This is how a phase (Ingestion, Transform) is shown moving through the spine.

ripple

The mouse disturbs the graph

Cursor proximity nudges nearby nodes; a click sends a ring outward. Reactive, not decorative. The graph acknowledges you, the way a live system would.

settle

Everything settles on a spring

Node returns, panel reveals, button presses all ease on cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1). Mass and settle, not snap. Buttons press down 2px and bounce back.
